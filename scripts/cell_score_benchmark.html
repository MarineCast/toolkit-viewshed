Scope and assumptions

Building inventory is assumed complete: no mapped footprint means zero building height. The 10 m grid retains native canopy detail, but terrain still has 30 m support. The 30 m candidate conservatively takes maximum obstacle height and any unknown support within each block. The fast direct-ray candidates evaluate one weighted water point per 2 × 2 block within each target cell, with a full census for cells containing fewer than 1,000 target pixels. Observer locations exclude mapped roof cells and retain an explicit 30 m canopy clearance.

Target cells retain their full water-pixel populations in the delivered raster model; boundary-clipped cells are excluded. The benchmark covers two source cells and a 5 km window. This is a model comparison, with no field acceptance or full regional rebuild. Timings use existing compiler caches; cold startup and raw input preparation are outside the calculation timings. Canopy crown crossings remain uncertain; no arbitrary transparency estimate is introduced.
