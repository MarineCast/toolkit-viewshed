## 6. From the map to the shoreline

What does this overhead grid feel like at eye level?

Start with the open view, then look through the trees. These two photographs give the combined map a human scale: a person stands at one spot, with a particular slice of water in front of them. The model summarizes multiple locations across the orange source cell.
### A · A view framed by trees The nearby branches screen part of the water, while the beach and a gap beyond remain visible. This is the kind of local variation the vegetation calculation tries to represent. Moving a short distance can change which paths are open; a single cell score cannot describe every viewing spot within it. [See the combined map again](#combined){ .md-button }

Use the photographs to read the landscape. They illustrate open water, foreground obstructions and the scale of the view. They do not establish the numerical weights, current vegetation, or the probability of spotting an animal. Photograph dates differ from the model's mixed canopy vintages.
