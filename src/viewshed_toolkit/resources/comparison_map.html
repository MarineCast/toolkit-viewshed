Why canopy remains partial

Victoria has supported clear observations, including 18 exact-point pairs with positive lower bounds, but no fully resolved positive pair. A representative unknown sightline depends on a mixed shoreline cell 60 m south of the observer (48.402843, −123.349061); its masked native subpixels are WorldCover water beside grassland. Dungeness has strong modeled canopy suppression, with a 14 m canopy dependency about 120 m north of the observer and masked built-up subpixels about 42 m northeast. These are model inputs and diagnostic dependencies, not field verification. Qualifying the native shoreline and built-up canopy heights is required for complete values; widening clearance or filling unknown heights with zero is not justified.

Source-cell comparison mean and uncertainty

The primary map averages canopy-aware binary LOS over five retained source-cell viewpoints and every retained 30 m water target center (stride 1). Each comparison has equal weight. If C are clear, B blocked/out of range and U unknown, total T=C+B+U; full mean bounds are C/T to (C+U)/T. A complete mean exists only when U=0. The known-only mean C/(C+B) describes resolved comparisons and does not estimate the unknown comparisons.

Target water samples are raster centers in the retained canonical water footprint, rather than a fixed 20-point design. Source samples are shown as red pins inside the outlined source grid cell. Terrain-only uses the same sample population. Distance remains a separate centroid diagnostic. Conditional vegetation and combined use the retained distance-integrated equivalent-area kernel, not this arithmetic denominator.

ETH 2020 maximum-resampled opaque canopy is the same at both sites. It does not represent crown gaps or canopy porosity. Exact-point diagnostics are secondary and use one observer. No source point was moved.

Method and scope

Values describe modeled physical support. The retained weighted terrain kernel already includes distance attenuation; the separate distance diagnostic is not multiplied into it. The primary terrain-only arithmetic visibility layer uses bare LOS without attenuation. The primary canopy mean uses actual source × target comparison counts; retained weighted kernels use equivalent water area. A pair outside the candidate set is not a zero. Native source-cell results average five observers; exact viewpoints have one observer. Vegetation is the conditional canopy ratio; combined includes terrain and canopy, without buildings. Unknown paths produce interval bounds; a pair value is available only when both bounds agree. Means of available pairs do not estimate missing pairs. Zero-baseline vegetation is not applicable. Its combined zero is forced by the baseline and is not canopy evidence. A vegetation value of zero means complete modeled canopy suppression, not an absence of trees. The observer’s private 30 m clearance is the existing modeling assumption, not evidence of open ground.

This pilot covers two source cells and their existing target envelopes. It does not establish regional coverage, absolute elevation accuracy, tides, urban obstruction or observer activity. The coastline is generalized for display only.
