What changed

Observer ground: the land observer uses the retained unflattened DEM independently of sea-level water targets. The exact points here did not need an elevation correction; a separate shoreline-sample regression exercises that fix.

Fine surfaces: ETH 2020 canopy is sampled at native nominal 10 m detail without 30 m maximum aggregation. Building footprints use 10 m cell centers. Terrain still has 30 m support. Grid origin stays fixed; the fine grid is nested in the retained grid. Direct rays traverse intersected cell interiors without the GDAL Edge horizon interpolation.

Canopy envelope: canopy has a zero floor and the predicted CHM ceiling. A crossing of canopy alone is uncertain because CHM supplies no trunks, crown bases or gap geometry. Predicted building roofs keep their modeled height. Missing canopy heights, missing roof heights and unverified building absence remain unknown. This assumes a grounded source cell and a 30 m canopy clearance around the observer; surrounding buildings are retained.

Limits: unweighted single-point sensitivity, distinct from the original five-sample H3 product and its distance-integrated kernel. No annual reconstruction, field validation, calibrated porosity or regional rebuild. Building imagery primarily represents 2019 with 2018 fill; canopy represents 2020. Finer rasterization can miss a small footprint at a cell center, so omitted footprints remain unknown.
