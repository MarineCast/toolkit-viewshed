# Viewshed Toolkit

<img class="viewshed-hero" src="assets/viewshed-toolkit-banner.png" alt="Ink panorama of coastal islands, a lookout and illustrated sightlines; decorative artwork, not a modeled result" width="2172" height="724">

<p class="viewshed-lead">Understand the <strong>physical limits of a view</strong> from coastal land and open water.</p>

Viewshed Toolkit models viewing support from **source areas** to **water target areas**. It uses
mapped land/water geometry, ground elevation, canopy where applicable, and explicit model
settings. The outputs are source–target tables with component scores, coverage states and
provenance.

A score describes **static physical viewability**. It is not animal presence, observer effort,
public access or the probability of seeing a whale. That distinction matters when using the
results to study observation bias or compare potential viewing locations.
